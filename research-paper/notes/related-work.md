# Related work to cite (replace weak dissertation refs)

Peer-reviewed anchors for each theme. Fill in exact bib entries when drafting §2.

## Sequential social dilemmas / emergent cooperation
- Leibo, Zambaldi, Lanctot, Marecki, Graepel (2017). *Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning
  in Sequential Social Dilemmas.* AAMAS. — closest prior work; reward structure → defect/
  cooperate. Cite as the main point of comparison.
- Perolat et al. (2017). *A multi-agent RL model of common-pool resource appropriation.*
  NeurIPS. — tragedy of the commons in MARL; directly relevant to the survival gridworld.
- Hughes et al. (2018). *Inequity aversion improves cooperation in intertemporal social
  dilemmas.* NeurIPS. — fairness/inequity angle.

## Algorithms / baselines
- Schulman et al. (2017). *Proximal Policy Optimization.* — already cited; keep.
- Yu et al. (2022). *The Surprising Effectiveness of PPO in Cooperative Multi-Agent Games
  (MAPPO).* NeurIPS D&B. — the centralised-critic baseline to position against.
- Lowe et al. (2017). *MADDPG.* NeurIPS. — CTDE reference.
- Foerster et al. (2018). *Counterfactual Multi-Agent Policy Gradients (COMA).* AAAI. —
  credit assignment; supports the mechanism argument.

## Game theory / fairness
- Axelrod (1984). *The Evolution of Cooperation.* — cooperation from self-interest.
- Jain, Chiu, Hawe (1984). *A quantitative measure of fairness.* — the Jain index source.

## Environments (contrast, not blogs)
- Terry et al. (2021). *PettingZoo.* NeurIPS D&B. — the API used; cite properly.
- Samvelyan et al. (2019). *SMAC.* AAMAS. — contrast (purely cooperative).
- Mordatch & Abbeel (2018) / OpenAI MPE. — contrast (no configurable reward regimes).
